Use AI-generated key takeaways?

To create a short takeaway for the filename, Papername sends the paper's title and abstract — not the PDF — to an AI service.

Papername does not store this content. The AI provider does not use API data for training by default, but may keep copies in safety logs for up to 30 days.

If the title or abstract is missing, Papername may use the paper's DOI to retrieve those details from Crossref.
